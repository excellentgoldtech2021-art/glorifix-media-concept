--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STEP-BY-STEP CPANEL UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Log in to your cPanel account (e.g. https://yourdomain.com:2083).
Step 2: Under the "Files" section, click on "File Manager".
Step 3: Double-click to open your web root folder:
        - For main domain: "public_html"
        - For addon domain / subdomain: public_html/your-subdomain-folder/
Step 4: Click the "Upload" button at the top toolbar.
Step 5: Select "glorifix_cpanel_ready.zip" from your Downloads folder:
        (Location on phone: Internal Storage -> Download -> glorifix_cpanel_ready.zip)
Step 6: Wait for the upload bar to reach 100% and turn green.
Step 7: Return to File Manager, right-click "glorifix_cpanel_ready.zip", and select "Extract".
Step 8: Confirm destination as "public_html" and click "Extract File(s)".
Step 9: (Optional) Delete "glorifix_cpanel_ready.zip" from public_html to save space.
